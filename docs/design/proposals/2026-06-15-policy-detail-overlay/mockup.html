PROPOSAL — Policy detail overlay + click/double-click/drag semantics. Today a single click on a policy card activates it, and the card only shows a 2-line description. This proposal splits inspecting from acting:

 •  Single click (or Enter/Space) → open the detail overlay below (full breakdown + an explicit action button).
 •  Double-click or drag to the other lane → enact / stop the policy (drag already works this way; double-click is the new shortcut).
 •  Recurring cards now show their lifespan honestly: "Runs until cancelled" (recurring policies have no fixed end in the engine).

Built from the live tokens. All three gestures are live in this mockup: single-click a card to open its overlay, double-click it to enact/stop (moves it to the other lane), or drag it across the divider. The buttons under the lanes preview each overlay state directly.
