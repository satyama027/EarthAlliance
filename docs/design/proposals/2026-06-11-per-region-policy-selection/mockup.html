Per-region policy selection — two lanes, drag to enact. Selecting a region scopes the board to it. The Available lane holds enactable policies; the Active lane holds what's running here. Drag a card up to enact it in this region; drag an active card back down (or hit its ✕) to remove it. Dragging an unaffordable card snaps it back and shows an error banner. Region: North America · costs scaled to its GDP share (~21%).
Removing an active policy: a buildout freezes at its current installed % (upkeep stops, the decarbonization it already bought persists); a recurring fund ends (upkeep and benefit both stop). A committed one-time policy is permanent — nothing to cancel — so it shows locked.
